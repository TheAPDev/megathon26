# Reference data sources

All figures are indicative as of October 2026 and must be verified before production use.

| File | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| cities.csv | 43 cities: NBC/ECBC climate zone, monthly mean temperature normals, PV specific yield, residential price per sq ft | IMD climatological normals (approx.), NBC 2016 / ECBC climate zone map, NREL/Global Solar Atlas yields (approx.), listing-portal price levels (approx.) |
| tariff_states.csv / tariff_slabs.csv | Domestic LT tariffs, fixed charges, duty, free-unit schemes, net-metering export rate, state solar top-ups | State ERC tariff orders FY2025-26 (approx.), desiutility.com, electricbilltool.com, state scheme notifications |
| emission_factors.csv | Grid and fuel CO2 factors | CEA CO2 Baseline Database v20; IPCC 2006 |
| subsidy_rules.csv | PM Surya Ghar central financial assistance | pmsuryaghar.gov.in; MNRE |
| finance_products.csv | Solar and green-home loan terms | SBI PM Surya Ghar loan page (Dec 2025); bank green home loan pages |
| appliances.csv | AC ISEER by star, fridge kWh, fan watts, efficiencies | BEE star labelling tables; literature |
| retrofit_catalogue.csv | India retrofit library, costs, effects | Market prices (approx.), BEE guidance (24 C), CLASP-BEE 2024 survey |
| zone_suitability.csv | Retrofit suitability by climate zone | ECBC / Eco-Niwas Samhita guidance, expert judgement |
